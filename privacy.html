Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Leadhoudini ("we", "us", "our") provides lead generation and patient acquisition services for med spas, aesthetic clinics, and other healthcare practices. This policy explains what information we collect through leadhoudini.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit the Book a Call form or complete the Free Marketing Scorecard, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number (phone is optional)
  • Your business name and industry
  • Your monthly marketing budget range and the challenge you describe
  • Your answers to each scorecard question, and the score we calculate from them

Booking information. When you schedule a call, Calendly collects the date and time you choose, your time zone, and your contact details. We receive a record of that booking.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider records technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device, and the pages you visit. If we run advertising or analytics tools, they may also use cookies or similar technologies to measure how people use the Site and how our ads perform (see section 5).

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare for your strategy call
  • To schedule, confirm, and run calls you book
  • To calculate and show your scorecard result, and to follow up about it
  • To contact you by email, phone, or text about our services. You can opt out at any time (see section 7)
  • To measure and improve the Site and our advertising
  • To keep the Site secure and prevent spam or abuse, and to meet legal obligations

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Site and our business, and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Google (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script) stores the details you submit in our lead records. Google Fonts may also receive your IP address when the Site loads.
  • Calendly handles call scheduling.
  • Vercel hosts the Site.
  • Advertising and analytics platforms such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google, if we use their tools on the Site.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a sale or merger of our business.

4. Health information

Our Site is for business owners and practice managers. Please do not send us patients' medical or other health information through our forms. If you work with us, we will agree separately how any patient-related data is handled.

5. Cookies and advertising technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our advertising partners may use cookies, pixels, and similar tools to understand Site traffic, measure which ads lead to enquiries, and show relevant ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage ad personalization through your Google and Meta ad settings. Blocking cookies may affect how parts of the Site work.

6. How long we keep it

We keep lead and booking information for as long as it is needed to follow up with you and run our business, and as required by law. You can ask us to delete it sooner (see section 7).

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. To unsubscribe from emails, use the link in the email or write to us. For texts, reply STOP. Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU and UK), you may have additional rights under privacy laws, including the right to know what we collect, to opt out of the sale or sharing of your information (we do not sell it), and to complain to a regulator. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

To make a request, email us at the address in section 11. We may need to confirm your identity first.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on our records. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may store and process information in the United States and other countries, which may have different data protection laws from yours. By using the Site you understand your information may be transferred there.

10. Children

The Site is intended for business use and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information:

  • Email: usamaahmed@leadhoudini.com
  • Instagram: @usamaahmedclinicmarketer
  • Facebook: Leadhoudini on Facebook

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
